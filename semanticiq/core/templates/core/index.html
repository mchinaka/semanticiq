The Problem

Industrial Software Reacts.
It Doesn't Understand.

Most industrial software is built around thresholds and alarms — it screams when a limit is breached. By then, efficiency has already dropped, energy has already been wasted, or equipment has already been stressed. The plant has data. What it lacks is understanding of what that data means against the physics of how the process should actually be behaving.

  • !Threshold-based alarms fire after the problem has already occurred — not before the conditions that cause it have developed.
  • !Energy waste is invisible until it hits the utility bill — processes drift from their design operating point for months with nobody watching the gap.
  • !Repair, replace, and capital planning decisions are made on age and gut feel — with no physics-grounded picture of remaining useful life or true degradation trajectory.
  • !Every site diagnoses the same failure modes alone — with no way to benchmark how similar equipment and processes perform elsewhere under comparable conditions.
